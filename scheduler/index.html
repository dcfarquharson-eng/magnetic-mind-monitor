Magnetic Mind Social Scheduler

Approve once.
Post everywhere, on time.

The Magnetic Mind Social Scheduler publishes the images and short videos you create and approve to your own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts, at the times you choose. Built for small businesses and creators who would rather run their business than post by hand every day.

How it works

1

Connect your accounts

Sign in on each platform's own login page and choose the Page, channel or account to post to. We never see your passwords.

2

Drop your content

Add your images or short videos and captions to your private folder. We prepare each one as a draft in your next open time slot.

3

Approve your week

See every upcoming post with its picture, caption, time and platforms. Approve, edit, reschedule or reject. Nothing posts without your approval.

4

We publish and report

Approved posts go out automatically at their scheduled time, with automatic retries. You get one short daily report of what was published.
