About The Institute

The Magnetic Mind Institute is a center for human potential, conscious leadership, and mindset transformation. We exist to help individuals dismantle inherited limitations, master the laws of consciousness, and step into their God-given power as the conscious directors of their lives.

Our Story

The foundation of The Magnetic Mind Institute was not conceived in a boardroom or borrowed from academic textbooks. It was forged through a forty-year personal journey—beginning at the pulpit of a Methodist church in Jamaica and culminating in an awakening inside a seminar hall in Scottsdale, Arizona.

At nineteen, Devon Farquharson answered the call to preach at Mount Edmondson Methodist Church in Jamaica, following in the footsteps of his father, a respected local preacher. Under the mentorship of church elders, he quickly rose to prominence, preaching throughout St. James, Westmoreland, Hanover, St. Elizabeth, and Trelawny, speaking at historic pulpits such as Coke Methodist Church in Kingston, and leading the Men's Fellowship across the county of Cornwall.

Yet deep within, an unyielding quest for truth began to collide with religious dogma. When a local primary school teacher requested baptism by immersion, Devon discovered in the historical writings of founder Charles Wesley that immersion was indeed recognized for adult converts. When her baptism took place, it sparked an intense institutional crisis.

Summoned before a disciplinary tribunal of twelve senior church leaders at St. John's Methodist Church in Montego Bay, Devon was pressured to conform to ecclesiastical authority. Instead, standing upon Joshua 24:15 and Acts 5:29, he reached into his bag, placed his clerical collar and preaching certificate upon the table, and made his decision:

"Today I choose to obey God rather than men." – Devon Farquharson, St. John's Methodist Church

He walked out of that room leaving his credentials behind. He had entered as a licensed lay preacher; he walked out as an authentic seeker of truth.

The Discovery of Paradigms

In the years that followed, Devon observed human nature closely. He took a position as an airline ticket agent with Express Air in Montego Bay, where he struck up a lifelong friendship with Sydney, an agent from rival carrier Trans Jamaica. Rooming together in Reading, the two spent their evenings in intensive biblical and historical study—free from pulpits, collection plates, or religious politics.

It became glaringly apparent that sincere people everywhere were held captive not by a lack of devotion or divine favor, but by unexamined subconscious paradigms. From calendar traditions established by ancient political councils to double standards that condemned the poor while excusing the wealthy, millions were living under inherited mental programs they had never once questioned.

Through these years of inquiry, Devon recognized the foundational truth that the thoughts we hold with feeling act as a magnet—drawing into our lives the circumstances, relationships, and "bridge of incidents" that match our dominant mental state.

The Scottsdale Awakening

Although career, material comfort, and financial success followed, Devon still felt an internal void. Life was still being experienced from the passenger seat—playing the role of an extra in his own story.

The profound shift occurred in March 2025 at a Proctor Gallagher seminar in Scottsdale, Arizona. Sitting in the audience at the Scottsdale Hilton, speaker Sandy Gallagher pointed directly in his direction and posed a single, reality-shattering question:

"Are you the director of your life, or are you just a part of the supporting cast?" – Sandy Gallagher, Scottsdale, Arizona

In an instant, decades of searching resolved into absolute clarity. He realized that subconscious paradigms had been directing his life while he sat passively waiting for external cues.

Immersing himself in Bob Proctor's signature curriculum, Thinking into Results, and the foundational Stick Person model originated by Dr. Thurman Fleet in 1934, Devon mastered the mechanics of mind: the conscious mind chooses the thought, but the subconscious mind governs emotional vibration and dictates physical results.

He stepped out of the audience, claimed the Director's chair, and rewrote the script of his life.

Our Mission Today

The Magnetic Mind Institute was established to bring this liberating knowledge to seekers, executives, and leaders worldwide. We teach individuals how to bridge the gap between knowing and doing, dismantle inherited paradigms, and consciously direct their health, relationships, and financial reality.
