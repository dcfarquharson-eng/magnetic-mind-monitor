Our Story

The Pulpit and The Stand in Jamaica

At nineteen, Devon Farquharson answered the call to preach at Mount Edmondson Methodist Church in Jamaica, following the footsteps of his father, a respected local preacher. Mentored by church elders, his ministry expanded rapidly across St. James, Westmoreland, Hanover, St. Elizabeth, and Trelawny, leading the Men's Fellowship across the county of Cornwall and speaking at historic pulpits including Coke Methodist Church in Kingston.

Yet deep within, an unyielding quest for truth collided with religious dogma. When a local schoolteacher requested baptism by immersion, Devon discovered in the writings of founder Charles Wesley that immersion was recognized for adult converts. Her baptism became a historic event, sparking an immediate theological backlash from the church hierarchy.

Summoned before a disciplinary council of twelve senior church leaders at St. John's Methodist Church in Montego Bay, Devon was pressured to recant. Instead, anchored in Joshua 24:15 and Acts 5:29, he reached into his bag, placed his clerical collar and preaching certificate upon the table, looked the council in the eyes, and declared:

"Today I choose to obey God rather than men." – Devon Farquharson, St. John's Methodist Church

Leaving his credentials behind, he walked out of that room. He entered as a licensed lay preacher; he walked out as an authentic seeker of truth.

The Freedom of Truth

"I walked into St. John's Methodist Church a lay preacher, and I walked out a man in search of the truth. My journey was not straight, and it was certainly not smooth. There were sharp curves, dangerous bends, and deep potholes every step of the way. But I decided then that if ever I preached again, it would not be because of credentials given to me by man."

Conviction • Integrity • Spiritual Freedom

Beyond Dogma: The Discovery of Paradigms

Outside the pulpit, Devon observed human nature closely. He took a position as an airline ticket agent with Express Air in Montego Bay, where he struck up a transformative friendship with Sydney from rival carrier Trans Jamaica. Rooming together in Reading, the two spent their evenings in intensive biblical and historical study—free from pulpits, collection plates, or religious politics.

Through these years of inquiry, it became unmistakable that sincere people everywhere were held captive not by a lack of devotion, but by unexamined subconscious paradigms. Millions were living under inherited mental programs—ancient calendar traditions, cultural conditioning, and uninspected rules—that they had never once paused to examine.

Devon recognized the foundational mental law of attraction and assumption: the thoughts we hold with feeling act as a magnet, drawing into our lives the circumstances, relationships, and 'bridge of incidents' that correspond to our inner state.

What is a Paradigm?

A paradigm is a mental program that has almost exclusive control over our habitual behavior—and almost all of our behavior is habitual.

As children, we had no conscious filter. What was repeated went straight into the subconscious mind, hardening into paradigms that dictate our adult income, relationships, and health. To change results, we must change the paradigm at the root.

The Scottsdale Awakening: Becoming The Director

Over the decades that followed, external success arrived: a thriving corporate career, financial security, and material comfort. Yet an internal void remained. Life was still being experienced from the passenger seat—playing the role of an extra waiting for someone else's cue.

The definitive shift occurred in March 2025 at a Proctor Gallagher seminar in Scottsdale, Arizona. Sitting among hundreds of attendees at the Scottsdale Hilton, speaker Sandy Gallagher pointed directly in his direction and asked the question that dismantled decades of conditioning:

"Are you the director of your life, or are you just a part of the supporting cast?" – Sandy Gallagher, Scottsdale, Arizona

In an instant, forty years of searching crystallized into absolute clarity. He had been an extra in his own movie, allowing subconscious paradigms to hold the camera. Devon committed entirely to Bob Proctor's signature curriculum, Thinking into Results, and the foundational Stick Person model originated by Dr. Thurman Fleet in 1934.

He understood the complete science of mind: the conscious mind chooses the thought, but the subconscious mind governs emotional vibration and dictates physical results. He stepped out of the audience, claimed the Director's chair, and rewrote the script of his life.

The Stick Person Framework

In 1934, Dr. Thurman Fleet created the Stick Person diagram because humanity lacked an image of the mind.

When you understand that the conscious mind chooses ideas, the subconscious mind generates emotional vibration, and the physical body is merely an instrument expressing that vibration, you hold the master key to personal transformation.
