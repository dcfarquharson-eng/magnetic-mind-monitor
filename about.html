About The Institute

The Magnetic Mind Institute is a center for human potential, conscious leadership, and mindset transformation. We exist to help individuals dismantle inherited mental limitations, master the laws of consciousness, and step into their God-given power as the conscious directors of their lives.

How It Started

The foundation of The Magnetic Mind Institute was not conceived in a boardroom or borrowed from academic textbooks. It was forged through a forty-year personal journey—beginning at the pulpit of a Methodist church in Jamaica and culminating in an awakening inside a seminar hall in Scottsdale, Arizona.

At nineteen, Devon Farquharson answered the call to preach at Mount Edmondson Methodist Church in Jamaica, following in the footsteps of his father, a respected local preacher. Under the mentorship of church elders, his ministry expanded rapidly across St. James, Westmoreland, Hanover, St. Elizabeth, and Trelawny. He was appointed head of the Men's Fellowship across the county of Cornwall and invited to speak at historic pulpits including Coke Methodist Church in Kingston.

Yet deep within, an unyielding quest for truth collided with religious dogma. When a local primary school teacher requested baptism by immersion, Devon discovered in the historical writings of founder Charles Wesley that immersion was indeed recognized for adult converts. When her baptism took place, it marked a historic first in Jamaica—and sparked an immediate, aggressive theological backlash from the church hierarchy.

Summoned before a disciplinary tribunal of twelve senior church leaders at St. John's Methodist Church in Montego Bay, Devon was pressured to conform to ecclesiastical authority. Instead, standing firmly upon Joshua 24:15 and Acts 5:29, he reached into his bag, placed his white clerical collar and preaching certificate upon the table, looked the council in the eyes, and declared:

"Today I choose to obey God rather than men." – Devon Farquharson, St. John's Methodist Church, Montego Bay

Leaving his credentials behind on that table, he walked out of that room. He entered as a licensed lay preacher; he walked out as an authentic seeker of truth, determined that if he ever preached again, it would not be because of credentials given by man.

In the years that followed, Devon took a position as an airline ticket agent with Express Air in Montego Bay. There he built a lifelong friendship with Sydney, an agent from rival carrier Trans Jamaica. Rooming together in Reading, the two spent their evenings in intensive biblical and historical study—free from pulpits, collection plates, or religious politics. Through those years of quiet inquiry, Devon uncovered the foundational truth of human conditioning: sincere people everywhere were held captive not by a lack of devotion or divine favor, but by unexamined subconscious paradigms—inherited mental programming passed down through generations without question.

Decades later, despite achieving corporate career success, financial security, and material comfort, an internal void remained. Life was still being experienced from the passenger seat—playing the role of an extra waiting for someone else's cue.

The definitive breakthrough occurred in March 2025 at a Proctor Gallagher seminar in Scottsdale, Arizona. Sitting among hundreds of attendees at the Scottsdale Hilton, speaker Sandy Gallagher pointed directly in his direction and asked the question that dismantled decades of conditioning:

"Are you the director of your life, or are you just a part of the supporting cast?" – Sandy Gallagher, Scottsdale, Arizona

In an instant, forty years of searching crystallized into absolute clarity. He had been an extra in his own movie, allowing subconscious paradigms to hold the camera. Devon committed entirely to Bob Proctor's signature curriculum, Thinking into Results, and the foundational Stick Person model originated by Dr. Thurman Fleet in 1934. He understood the complete science of mind: the conscious mind chooses the thought, but the subconscious mind governs emotional vibration and dictates physical results. He stepped out of the audience, claimed the Director's chair, and rewrote the script of his life.

Where We Are Today

Today, The Magnetic Mind Institute serves individuals, executives, and leaders worldwide who are ready to bridge the gap between knowing and doing. We provide structured coaching, mastermind environments, and proven mental technology rooted in the Proctor Gallagher lineage.

Through our signature masterclasses, personal mentorship, and the *Thinking into Results* framework, we guide clients to identify and dismantle the hidden paradigms holding them back—helping them achieve quantum leaps in their health, wealth, relationships, and purposeful impact.

Where We Are Going

Our vision is to build an international ecosystem for conscious growth and personal mastery. We are actively expanding our global footprint through cutting-edge digital platforms, daily personal development tools, executive retreats, and our mobile applications.

We are dedicated to reaching millions of individuals who feel that deep internal stirring—those who know they were meant for more than settling for an uninspected script. At The Magnetic Mind Institute, our destination is clear: to empower a global community of conscious creators who step fully into the Director's chair of their lives.
