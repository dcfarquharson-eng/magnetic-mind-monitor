## Part 1: The Stick Person (A Picture of the Mind)

The mind is an activity, not a physical organ. The brain is merely the physical instrument of the mind, just as the piano is the instrument of the musician.

Because no one has ever seen the mind, whenever people think of their mind, they either picture their brain or a blank void. In 1934, a brilliant chiropractor and healer named **Dr. Thurman Fleet** recognized this problem. He realized that without a clear image to work with, confusion is inevitable.

He drew a simple diagram that has transformed millions of lives worldwide: **The Stick Person**.

```
       +-----------------------+
       |   1. CONSCIOUS MIND   |   <--- The Thinking Mind (Intellect)
       |   (Accepts / Rejects) |
       +-----------------------+
       | 2. SUBCONSCIOUS MIND  |   <--- The Emotional Mind (Paradigms)
       |   (Accepts Everything)|
       +-----------------------+
                   |
                   |
            +-------------+
            |   3. BODY   |        <--- The Physical Instrument (Action & Vibration)
            +-------------+
```

Notice that the head is drawn disproportionately large. That is because the head represents the **Mind**, which controls everything the physical body does.

## Part 2: The Two Halves of the Mind

The mind operates in two distinct compartments:

### 1. The Conscious Mind (The Thinking Mind)
* **What it is:** Your educated, intellectual mind. This is the part of you reading and evaluating these words right now.
* **Its Superpower:** The conscious mind possesses the ability to **accept or reject** any idea. You can choose your thoughts. You can choose to believe an idea, or you can reject it completely.
* **The Trap:** The conscious mind holds your ideas, goals, and logic, but **it does not control your habitual behavior.**

### 2. The Subconscious Mind (The Emotional Mind)
* **What it is:** Your subconscious mind is the power center. It controls your heartbeat, your digestion, your involuntary nervous system, and **95% of your daily habitual actions.**
* **Its Vulnerability:** The subconscious mind **cannot reject** anything. It accepts whatever is impressed upon it as absolute truth. It cannot differentiate between what is real and what is vividly imagined.
* **Where Your Results Live:** Whatever idea is repeatedly impressed upon the subconscious mind becomes fixed in your neural programming. This cluster of fixed habits is called your **Paradigm**.

## Part 3: The Universal Chain of Results

Everything in your physical world—your bank balance, your relationships, your health, and your daily habits—follows an unbroken universal law:

$$\text{Thoughts} \longrightarrow \text{Feelings} \longrightarrow \text{Vibration} \longrightarrow \text{Action} \longrightarrow \text{Results}$$

1. **Thoughts (Conscious Mind):** You choose an idea in your thinking mind.
2. **Feelings (Subconscious Mind):** When you emotionally involve yourself with that idea, it sinks into your subconscious mind.
3. **Vibration (The Body):** The emotional state of your subconscious mind dictates the physical vibration of every cell in your body.
4. **Action:** Your physical vibration dictates how you act, speak, move, and react to the world around you.
5. **Results:** Your actions attract corresponding circumstances, people, and opportunities.

Most people try to change their results by forcing themselves into new **Actions** (working more hours, grinding, willpower). But because their subconscious **Paradigm** remains unchanged, their old programming pulls them right back to their old results within weeks.

**To permanently change your results, you must change the Paradigm.**

## Part 4: The 3 Daily Repetition Exercises

Because your subconscious mind cannot reject an idea, there are two primary ways a paradigm is formed: **an emotional shock** or **spaced repetition**. 

Here are the 3 daily exercises used at The Magnetic Mind Institute to consciously rewrite your subconscious programming:

### Exercise 1: The Morning Goal Card Routine
1. Write your primary goal on a physical 3x5 index card in the present tense:
   > *"I am so happy and grateful now that [Your Specific Goal Achieved in Present Tense]."*
2. Keep this card in your pocket at all times.
3. Every time your hand touches that card throughout the day, take 5 seconds to visualize the completed result and feel the emotion of already having it.

### Exercise 2: Conscious Thought Guarding
1. Whenever a negative thought enters your conscious mind (doubt, fear, limitation), remember your conscious superpower: **You have the ability to reject it.**
2. Say quietly to yourself: *"Cancel that thought."*
3. Immediately replace it with the polar opposite empowering thought. Never allow an idea of failure to linger long enough to evoke an emotional feeling.

### Exercise 3: The Evening Subconscious Imprint
1. The last 10 minutes before you fall asleep are the most critical of your entire day. Your brain transitions into Alpha and Theta brainwave states, where the subconscious mind is wide open.
2. Never watch stressful news, check work emails, or rehash daytime problems in bed.
3. Spend those 10 minutes visualizing your goal as an accomplished fact. Drift off to sleep feeling the deep gratitude and peace of that reality. Your subconscious mind will process that imprint for 8 hours while you rest.
